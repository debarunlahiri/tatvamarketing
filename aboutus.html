About Us

Established in year 2007, “ Tatva Marketing & Services Pvt. Ltd.” is a leading organization in India, Primarily focuses on "Ultrasonic Testing Equipments" & majorly occupied in distributing, servicing and providing qualitative assortment of Ultrasonic Thickness Gauges, Ultrasonic Accessories, Ultrasonic Rail Testers, MPI Equipments, Electro Magnetic Crack Detectors, MPI Equipment etc, for various industries like Aircraft / Airlines, Indian Railways, Aviation, Steel / Metal Industries & various other Industries. Our Clients are a big reputed organisations like Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd, Indian Railways (Eastern, Western, Northern, Southern), NTPC Ltd, Delhi Metro Rail Corporation Ltd, ABG Shipyard Ltd, Godrej & Boyce Mfg. Co. Ltd, Grasim Industries Ltd, Jindal Strips Ltd, Jubilant Organosys Ltd, L&T Komatsu Ltd, Nuclear Power Corporation of India Ltd, Essar Steel Ltd & Many More. In order to store our products safely, we have constructed a well-equipped warehousing base that is administered by warehousing personnel and storekeepers. Owing to our timely delivery, affordable price range, excellent logistics facility and client-oriented approach, we have acquired huge client base across the nation.

We are associated with reliable vendors of the market. Our vendors manufacture these products using high grade components and cutting-edge technology. The selection process for the reliable vendor is done by our procuring agents on the basis of their production base, delivery schedule, market credibility, production techniques, product quality, track record, etc.
